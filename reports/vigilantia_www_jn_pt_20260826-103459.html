Alto

R02 Tracking/analytics cookies are set before the user has given any consent.

Alto

RecomendaçãoConfigure the tag manager/CMP to only fire tracking scripts after explicit opt-in consent.

Evidência Foram encontrados cookies de rastreio antes da obtenção de consentimento.

R03 A Google Analytics / gtag.js script was detected without IP anonymization enabled.

Médio

RecomendaçãoEnable IP anonymization (anonymize_ip) in the analytics configuration, or use a privacy-friendly analytics tool.

Evidência Foi detetado Google Analytics sem anonimização de IP.

R04 One or more cookies are missing the Secure and/or HttpOnly attributes.

Médio

RecomendaçãoSet the Secure and HttpOnly flags on all cookies that do not need to be read by client-side JavaScript.

Evidência Foram encontrados cookies sem atributos Secure/HttpOnly adequados.

R07 The privacy policy text does not mention the right to erasure ('right to be forgotten').
